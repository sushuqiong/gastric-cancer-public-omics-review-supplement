## Notes

- All scans are supportive landscape evidence for a narrative review, not PRISMA systematic-review products.
- The supplementary package, including the search logs, PMID-level record sets, coding rules, checklist, and generation scripts, is archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23066112 and is cited in the Data Availability Statement.
- Supplementary Table S5 was rebuilt in this revision after every cited study was re-examined against its full text.
- Supplementary Table S4 was restructured in this revision: on re-checking, 121 of 135 records that had been flagged as probably absent from PubMed proved to be indexed there.
